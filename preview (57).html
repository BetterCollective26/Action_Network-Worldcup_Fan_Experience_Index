Global Fan Experience Ranking

Vancouver is the clear winner in the global table, finishing 19 points ahead of Toronto and nearly 25 points ahead of Seattle. The Canadian host city performs especially well on walkability, hotel density, bar density and match-day weather.

The global ranking also shows how different match-day strengths can offset weaknesses. Mexico City and Monterrey score well on cost, while Atlanta benefits from the shortest listed journey time to the stadium. However, cities with long transit routes, lower walkability, low nearby hotel density or hotter weather tend to fall down the final table.

U.S. Host City Ranking

When the 11 U.S. host cities are ranked separately, Seattle leads with a score of 73.0. Its high walkability, strong bar density, relatively mild weather and hotel availability near Lumen Field make it the strongest U.S. fan experience in the data.

Atlanta ranks second among U.S. cities thanks to its 27-minute listed stadium journey time and strong walkability, while Los Angeles takes third. At the bottom, Miami and Kansas City face the toughest fan-experience profile among U.S. hosts, with lower walkability and limited nearby stadium-area density.

What Separates the Best Host Cities?

The strongest cities do not just perform well in one area. Vancouver combines elite walkability, a cooler match-day temperature profile and the best hotel density in the analysis, while Toronto stands out for nearby bar density and Seattle performs strongly across the U.S. set.

The data also shows why affordability alone is not enough. Mexico City, Monterrey and Guadalajara are among the most affordable cities for food and beer, but lower stadium-area density and longer journeys prevent them from topping the overall table.

Methodology

The Fan Experience Index ranks all 16 FIFA 2026 host cities using objective, stadium-level data across five categories: accessibility, cost, atmosphere, safety and weather, and accommodation. Each metric is normalized to a 0–10 scale using min-max normalization, then weighted and summed to create a final Fan Experience Score out of 100.

Lower-is-better metrics, including journey time, meal cost, beer price and match-day temperature, are inverted so lower raw values improve a city’s score. Stadium-level data is used throughout so the ranking reflects the actual match-day area rather than the broader branded host city.

Accessibility25%

Journey time and walkability around the stadium.

Cost20%

Average meal cost and average beer price.

Atmosphere20%

Nearby bar density (places per sq. mi.) and football pitches per 100,000 residents.

Safety & Weather20%

Safety score and average match-day temperature.

Accommodation15%

Nearby hotel density, shown as places per sq. mi. within the stadium-area measure.
