03Score drivers

What Separates the Best Host Cities?

The strongest cities do not just perform well in one area. Vancouver combines elite walkability, a cooler matchday temperature profile and the best hotel density in the analysis, while Toronto stands out for nearby bar density and Seattle performs strongly across the U.S. set.

The data also shows why affordability alone is not enough. Mexico City, Monterrey and Guadalajara are among the most affordable cities for food and beer, but lower stadium-area density and longer travel times prevent them from topping the overall table.

04Methodology

Methodology

The Fan Experience Index ranks all 16 FIFA 2026 host cities using objective, stadium-level data across five categories: accessibility, cost, atmosphere, safety and weather, and hotel access. Each metric is normalized to a 0–10 scale using min-max normalization, then weighted and summed to create a final Fan Experience Score out of 100.

Lower-is-better metrics, including travel time, meal cost, beer price and matchday temperature, are inverted so lower raw values improve a city’s score. Stadium-level data is used throughout so the ranking reflects the actual matchday area rather than the broader branded host city.

Accessibility25%

Measures how simple it is for fans to get to and move around the stadium area.

  • Travel time: estimated city-center-to-stadium journey time.
  • Walkability: how easy the surrounding area is to navigate on foot.
Cost20%

Tracks the everyday matchday spending fans are most likely to feel.

  • Meal cost: average price of a casual meal in the host area.
  • Beer price: average beer cost, with lower prices improving the score.
Atmosphere20%

Looks at the social and soccer culture around the matchday area.

  • Bar density: nearby bars and pub-style venues per sq. mi.
  • Soccer fields: local soccer footprint per 100,000 residents.
Safety & Weather20%

Balances general safety conditions with expected summer matchday heat.

  • Safety score: overall city safety indicator used for comparison.
  • Matchday temp: average expected temperature around game day.
Hotel Access15%

Shows how much accommodation choice fans have close to the stadium.

  • Hotel density: nearby hotel options per sq. mi.
  • Stadium-area measure: focuses on the matchday base, not the wider city.
Final Index Score0–100

Combines the weighted categories into one overall fan experience score.

  • Normalize & invert: metrics are scored 0–10, with lower-is-better data flipped.
  • Weight & scale: category scores are combined and converted to a 0–100 index.
